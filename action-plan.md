# Semester Project: West Point Hotel

My project this semester is to design and develop a website for a new luxury hotel located on a Caribbean island. The goal is to create a website that not only reflects the hotel’s luxury positioning, but also strategically supports its launch and long-term growth.

## How will I perfect this project?

I plan on focusing on creating the 'dine' pages for the restaurant. These pages will include a banner, feature image, description, and button to see the menu. I will also work on the experiences page which will include images, a description, and a carousel to scroll through pictures on each activity.
