Semester Project: West Point Hotel

Team Name: Pixel Point
Team Members: Clare, Carolina, Alyssa

My project this semester is to design and develop a website for a new luxury hotel located on a Caribbean island. The goal is to create a website that not only reflects the hotel’s luxury positioning, but also strategically supports its launch and long-term growth.

How will I perfect this project?

I plan on focusing on the communications side of branding. This will include working closely with the client to develop a clear mission statement and define their core values. Establishing this foundation will give us a roadmap to guide all website content and promotional materials. It will also ensure consistency in tone, messaging, and overall brand identity across the site and social media platforms.

I also plan to manage our group workflow. As we receive new assignments, I will break each deliverable into clear sections and assign internal deadlines for completion. I will then organize these tasks on our Kanban board to keep everything structured and visible. After organizing the workflow, I will update the team and we will divide responsibilities among ourselves to ensure balance and efficiency. We will consider time constraints and availability, as well as strengths and weaknesses as we assign the tasks.

Implementation:

To prepare for these responsibilities, I will research mission statements and brand messaging from established luxury hotels that align with our long-term vision. I will analyze how their messaging communicates exclusivity and experience and use those insights to help me develop a mission statement. I will look for a balance between reflecting both industry standards and the new hotel identity. I will also meet with the client to review my findings, gather feedback, and ensure that all messaging decisions remain aligned with their vision and goals.
